# Thesis Details

## Project Title

**Rice Paddy Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) Monitoring and Snail Habitat Risk Management System**

## Primary Data Fields in the Record Table

1. **Record Date and Time** – The monitoring month, day of the week, and observation hour.
2. **Crop Growth Stage** – The current rice production stage, such as transplanting.
3. **Water-Table Depth (cm)** – The measured depth of the water table in the paddy.
4. **Soil Moisture (VWC %)** – The volumetric water-content percentage of the soil.
5. **Snail Habitat Risk** – The assessed risk level for snail habitat, such as Low, Moderate, or High.
6. **AWD Gate Action** – The recommended or recorded gate-control action for managing field water.

## Three Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the Admin

1. **Total Monitoring Records** – The number of field observations recorded in the system, indicating monitoring coverage.
2. **High Snail-Habitat-Risk Records** – The number of observations classified as high risk, helping the admin prioritize intervention.
3. **AWD Gate Actions Required** – The number of records that require a gate-control action, indicating the current water-management workload.

These fields and KPIs allow the admin to monitor field conditions, identify areas with elevated snail-habitat risk, and track the operational actions needed to support efficient AWD water management.
